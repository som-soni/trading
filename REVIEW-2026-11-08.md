## 1. Verdict in short

- **Stored data is the biggest risk this week.** The split repair code corrupted AQYLON.NS and hides
  BANSALWIRE.NS's fake -80% gap. Four India ETFs keep fake two-bar price runs, and one of them gave
  etf_swing a -43.9R trade. The US results blackout drops the real results date (found by three
  reviewers), so every Minervini 1.52+ backtest under-blocks results.
- **The new books, positions and paper code gives wrong instructions on real paths.** Splits are
  ignored, the take form drops the strategy's exit settings, and a partial sale is asked for twice.
  The pure tests pass. Nothing is wrong yet only because no real position exists.
- **India's indices and benchmark are still a session behind.** Last week's cutoff fix landed, but
  the provider still drops the day's bar on an evening run. India paper books are stalled, and a
  lag at month-end would skip the rebalance silently.
- **The hold-out discipline has holes.** The verify-once gate keys on the version label, and it
  counts portfolio options the runner throws away. 18 of 22 verify verdicts were never recorded.
  Several statuses say "not yet measured" or lead with legacy wins after a failed verify.
- **Since last review:** both fix branches are merged. Of 137 status rows, 38 are fixed and 77 are
  still open. Last week's Fix now 4 happened again: a manual download held the main lane for 26 h
  and US open started 24.5 h late.
- **Trader experience: the decision does not travel between pages.** A sized, recorded trade takes
  about 12 clicks and 6 page changes, and the run's size, stop and results date are retyped or
  missing on the way, so carrying them into Today and the Take form comes first (section 6).
- **Strategy and risk: sizing, not stock picking, drives most of the losses.** Every book sizes on
  the full account and cash-starved part fills hold the big winners, so the first steps are a share
  of one account per book, an account heat cap and a minimum fill test (section 7; optimise stage
  only, nothing backtested yet).

the strategy's Candidates (opened on Qualified, sorted A to Z), the chart, Analysis › Entry to size

## 7. Strategy, risk and sizing

On the optimise-stage runs, more of the losses come from how the book is sized than from which
stocks it picks. In the cash-bound US books a third to half of the trades fill below half their
planned size, and those part fills carry many of the large R winners, so a +5R trade earns little
money, while tight stops push other positions to the 25% cap where gaps through the stop hurt. No
risk is set across books at all: every book sizes on the full account, so eight US books stacked
carry 39% average open risk (57% at the peak), while the same eight split one share each made 7.33%
a year at a -17.2% drawdown.

in-sample. Nothing comes from a verify run, and none of these proposals has been backtested yet: the
effects are estimates from stored trades. Write each pass criterion down before its run.

## Appendix C: rejected findings

- **Kullamägi retuned the shared Big movers threshold.** Only kullamagi uses that screen, the change was version-bumped and documented, and studies recompute live.
- **Kullamägi G4 note divides by a zero ADR.** numpy float division gives nan with a warning, not an exception.
- **Box pyramid rests the add over an old box.** DV_TOP is set only on the box's completion row, so an old box is never seen.
- **Paper fill not atomic, so a crash fills twice.** Every fill path re-derives from holdings, so a re-run finds the trade done.
- **stage_analysis watch flags stop trades.** Capping the tier from watch flags is the documented base contract, shared by other strategies.
- **End-of-day fill skips days above 80% coverage.** The missing bars are SME, REIT and InvIT series the file never maps, even on filled days.
- **momentum_vol_scaled headline reads as an edge.** The status opens with the failed v1.9 verify, and the badge is neutral.
- **Take and exit dates default to the UTC day.** The UTC day matches both markets' session dates for an India-based user; the proposed `todayLocal()` fix would break US fills.
