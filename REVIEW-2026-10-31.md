the whole repo across 17 review areas. 18 reviewers each sent their findings to an independent
verifier: 170 findings survived (164 CONFIRMED, 6 PLAUSIBLE) and 3 were rejected. Every test module

## 1. Verdict in short

- **Last week's two fix branches never reached main.** The first-paint fix
  (`refs/heads/claude/mystifying-mccarthy-380bbb`) and the backtest-accuracy fix
  (`refs/heads/claude/clever-kare-663726`) sit unmerged. The browser still waits about 29 s for
  `/api/status` on a cold load, and five confirmed backtest bugs are still live on main.
- **Backtest results now depend on the signal cache.** Minervini 1.38 builds a different plan on a
  fresh scan than on a cache hit, so the same run can trade differently (HIGH).
- **Several labels that steer a trading decision are wrong.** An edge badge reads "Edge
  out-of-sample" for a verify run that failed its criterion. Six strategy statuses quote stale or
  unstaged figures. India's indices and benchmark lag a session after the evening price run.
- **Monday 2 Nov needs one fix first.** From that day the US open and India close routines fire in
  the same minute and US open queues behind (`scripts/jobs/queue.py:101`).
- **Operations risks grew.** There is still no Postgres backup, the signal cache is back to 99 GB
  four days after a truncate, and the superuser password sits in a world-readable plist.

   start a long non-schedule item that would overrun the next due schedule slot.

   close (18:30 IST) land in the same minute from 2 Nov, and US open queues behind a routine that
